How It Works

From report to resolution in four clear steps.

    Report

    Tell us about the civic issue.

  2. 02

    Review

    The complaint is reviewed by the responsible authority.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    The issue is assigned and addressed.

  4. 04

    Track

    Citizens can monitor the progress until resolution.

About CivicConnect

CivicConnect is a concept platform designed to make communication between citizens and local authorities easier and more transparent.

Our mission is to make civic services more accessible, transparent, and citizen-friendly.

CivicConnect is a Class 12 student project and concept platform. It is a student project prototype and is not an official government service. No data leaves your browser.

  • Transparency

    Clear status at every stage of a complaint.

  • Accessibility

    Usable by keyboard, screen reader and on any screen size.

  • Community Participation

    Every citizen can raise and follow local issues.
